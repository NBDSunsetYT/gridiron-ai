## Source and cutoff interpretation

A source's retrieval time is not its publication time. A successful immutable capture **before a future cutoff** does prove that the captured edition existed by then. Original publisher timestamps stay null when unknown. This exception cannot recover historical publication evidence from a document first downloaded after an old game.

The current NBA injury report was issued **2026-11-06 23:15 UTC**, Last-Modified **23:15:04 UTC**, and retrieved **23:23:41 UTC**. The initial parser failed; the repaired parser successfully recovered all eight pages. Seven team-report rows remained unsubmitted. The historical 2024 report was retrieved separately and retains its distinct source hash; it is not a current lineup source.
