# OCR assets

Tesseract.js 7.0.0 and tesseract.js-core assets are Apache-2.0 licensed. Their notices are included beside the assets. English tessdata_fast model source: https://tessdata.projectnaptha.com/4.0.0_fast/eng.traineddata.gz (Tesseract tessdata_fast, Apache-2.0; https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast).

Screenshots are processed locally; these assets are served by the application. OCR output requires explicit user verification and is never treated as an authenticated market feed.
